About Dr Sultan Zaidi

Dr Syed Sultan Ahmed Zaidi is a UK-certified practitioner of Dry Needling and Dynamic Cupping, and is also CORU licensed (Ireland). He practices advanced therapeutic techniques that offer targeted pain relief, improved mobility, and accelerated recovery for a wide range of conditions. Dr. Sultan Zaidi also serves as the Incharge of the Physiotherapy Department at Medicare Cardiac and General Hospital, reflecting his strong clinical leadership and expertise in rehabilitation care.
