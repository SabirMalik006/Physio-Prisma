About Prisma Healthcare

Prisma Healthcare is an integrated healthcare organization founded in Pakistan. We specialize in combining high-standard clinical care, professional healthcare education, and the supply of world-class medical & rehabilitation equipment.

Founder & CEO

Dr. Tuba Sultan

Consultant Physiotherapist | Clinical Researcher

Dr. Tuba Sultan is a Consultant Physiotherapist graduated from the United College of Physical Therapy, a Certified Aesthetic Practitioner, Clinical Researcher, and licensed ACLS & BLS provider. As the Founder & CEO of Prisma Healthcare, she is dedicated to delivering ethical, evidence-based, and patient-centered rehabilitation care.

Her clinical expertise spans pain management and rehabilitation across orthopedic, musculoskeletal, neurological, gynaecological, pediatric, and geriatric conditions. Beyond direct patient care, Dr. Tuba actively ensures that both professionals and patients have access to clinically reliable treatment solutions and high-quality equipment.

She is equally passionate about clinical education and has developed a learning platform that promotes practical training and continuous professional education through hands-on learning initiatives.

Co-Founder & Director

Dr. Syed Sultan Ahmed Zaidi

Rehabilitation Specialist | Consultant Physiotherapist

Dr. Syed Sultan Ahmed Zaidi is a dedicated Consultant Physiotherapist from Ziauddin University and a UK-certified practitioner of Dry Needling and Dynamic Cupping. He currently serves at Medicare Cardiac & General Hospital as a senior consultant.

With over four years of hands-on clinical experience in musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiac, orthopaedic, and sports rehabilitation, he is committed to helping patients regain mobility and improve quality of life through tailored, evidence-based care.

As Director of Prisma Healthcare, Dr. Zaidi focuses on clinical excellence and developing learning platforms that bridge the gap between academic knowledge and clinical skill development for the next generation of healthcare professionals.

"Driven by clinicians who practice what they preach"
